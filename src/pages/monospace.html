What is a monospace font?

A monospace font is one where each character is the same width as all the others.

When to use monospace?

  • Numbers that change often
  • Timers
  • Clocks

When you don't have to use monospace:

  • Numbers that do not change
  • Numbers that change every couple of minutes
  • Greeble numbers (But even then you are probably better off using monospace)
